Place your image files in this folder:

  hero.jpg         — Full-width background photo for the hero section
  logo.png         — Restaurant logo (transparent background preferred)
  logo.svg         — SVG version of logo if available
  special-1.jpg    — Photo for first special card
  special-2.jpg    — Photo for second special card
  special-3.jpg    — Photo for third special card

After adding images, update index.html and css/styles.css:
  - In index.html: uncomment the <img> tag in the nav and special-card sections
  - In css/styles.css: the hero background-image is already set to url('../images/hero.jpg')
